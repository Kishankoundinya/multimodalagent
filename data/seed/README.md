# Seed Documents Directory

Place your own legally obtained company annual reports (PDF format) in this directory (`data/seed/`).

### Recommended Document Types:
- Publicly available 10-K filings (e.g., Apple, Microsoft, Alphabet SEC 10-K filings).
- Company Integrated Annual Reports or ESG reports in PDF format.

### Automatic Detection:
When you place PDF documents in this directory, the **Financial Research AI** backend will automatically detect them. You can review them in the Documents page or Dashboard and index them with a single click.

*Note: For privacy and copyright compliance, do not commit proprietary documents to source control.*
